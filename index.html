About

SayBoard is free and works offline. Nothing leaves this device: no accounts, no tracking. Picture symbols: ARASAAC (arasaac.org). Author: Sergio Palao. Owner: Government of Aragón, Spain. License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, so SayBoard stays free and non-commercial. A few buttons use original SayBoard drawings or Mulberry Symbols (Steve Lee, CC BY-SA 4.0).
